Where your sound goes

Movie Sound sits between your browser and your DAC (here an iFi Pro iDSD). Shaded steps change the sound.

Movie Sound on (the Mac's output is BlackHole)
Safari→Mac output: BlackHole, 2 channels→Movie Sound: your EQ preset, leveler, limiter→iFi
processed once
Chrome, Tidal extension's EQ off for that tab→BlackHole→Movie Sound→iFi
processed once, same as Safari
Chrome, Tidal extension's EQ on→extension's EQ→BlackHole→Movie Sound→iFi
processed twice: bass +13 dB becomes about +26 dB
Movie Sound off (the Mac's output is the iFi)
Safari→iFi
untouched
Chrome, Tidal extension's EQ on→extension's EQ→iFi
processed once
Where Movie Sound gets its EQ settings
Tidal extension: presets and sliders→saved in Chrome→its link script, on the Movie Sound page only→Movie Sound
follows live
  • Pick a preset or move a slider in the extension and Movie Sound changes with it, whether or not you save the preset.
  • The extension's AUDIO EQ on/off switch isn't followed. Turning it off to avoid the double processing above leaves Movie Sound on, so the film is still processed once.
  • To pick a preset on the Movie Sound page itself, untick Follow Chrome there. Tick it again to go back to following.
  • Pitch stays off, as the extension does for video, so the picture and sound stay in sync.
  • Without the extension (or with a version before 1.5.7), Movie Sound uses the presets saved in bridge/chrome-settings.js.

BlackHole has 2 channels, so the browser folds 5.1 or Atmos down to stereo before Movie Sound hears it. The Stream Quality HUD isn't on any of these paths: it only reads facts about the video, so turning it on or off changes nothing you hear.
